Profile

Professional positioning

I am an M.S. candidate at MIPT working across LLM post-training, applied ML, and optimization research. My strongest applied case is Qwen/DeepScaleR math distillation: 40.3k teacher CoT traces, SFT/Forward-KL training, evaluation on five math benchmarks, and macro Pass@1 improvement from 46.97 to 54.45 against a 55.90 local RL teacher.

At MIPT AI Institute / BRAIn Lab I worked as a Research Team Lead: I led small teams on distillation/SFT, LLM compression, multi-agent RL/NLHF, SGD dynamics, and optimization tracks. My role was hypothesis framing, experiment decomposition, code review, and baseline/evaluation discipline.

  • LLM post-training: SFT, teacher/KL distillation, synthetic and teacher-generated data.
  • Evaluation: graders, reward parsing, benchmark tables, baseline and teacher comparison.
  • Research leadership: distillation/SFT, LLM compression, multi-agent RL/NLHF, optimization tracks.
  • ML engineering: Python, C++, SQL, PyTorch, Hugging Face workflows, Linux, Git.
  • Mathematics: optimization, stochastic processes, minimax problems, probability theory.
  • Data workflows: JSONL, YQL, DataLens, Nirvana.

Experience

Research and industry work

2024 - 2026 | MIPT AI Institute / BRAIn Lab / MIRAI; ongoing collaboration with LAB MMO

Research Team Lead / LLM Post-Training Engineer

I led five small research teams of 1-3 people and also contributed technically to LLM/post-training projects. My scope included research framing, baseline selection, task decomposition, code and experiment review, metric control, and preparation of reproducible artifacts.

Qwen / DeepScaleR math distillation

Built an end-to-end research pipeline for transferring part of the DeepScaleR-1.5B-Preview RL gain back into DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Qwen-1.5B through teacher-generated CoT, SFT, and Forward KL. The workflow covered vLLM teacher generation, reward/grader parsing, cleaning, train/val split, benchmark evaluation, and summary tables. Result: 40,300 teacher traces; macro Pass@1 on AIME 2024, MATH500, AMC 2023, Minerva Math, and OlympiadBench improved from 46.97 to 54.45, with a 55.90 local RL teacher.

Multi-agent RL / NLHF / HeteroCrossPlay

Led a research track on preference learning where chosen/rejected pairs are produced by interaction between multiple models rather than only by a static preference dataset. My contribution: NLHF/cross-play framing, connecting game theory with LLM alignment, verifier-reward framing, comparison with self-play, DPO/PPO/GRPO-style approaches, and experimental setup around a modified VERL recipe.

LLM compression / pruning / healing

Led a research track on structural redundancy in decoder-only LLMs: layer pruning, LoRA healing, decoder/layer importance search, and attention-head merging. My contribution: setting the baseline -> compression -> healing -> evaluation workflow, reviewing experiments, and interpreting quality/size trade-offs.

Optimization and SGD dynamics tracks

Led and contributed to projects on finite-step SGD dynamics, minimax/DRO optimization, Frank-Wolfe variants, and applied optimization. These are supporting, not core NLP projects, but they strengthen hypothesis design, baseline comparison, and stability analysis for training objectives.
