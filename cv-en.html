Profile

Current profile

I am an M.S. candidate at MIPT working across optimization research and practical ML systems. My strongest engineering work is in experiment pipelines, evaluation, reproducibility, and infrastructure around LLM training and inference ideas rather than in large-scale production ownership of a single commercial system.

Selected Projects

What the project work actually looks like

SGDiffusion

A repository for finite-step SGD experiments. I use it to run controlled trajectory experiments, compare them against Langevin-style approximations, inspect Hessian-basis behavior, and regenerate paper figures. This is experiment and analysis code, not a productized training framework.

Ridiculous-LLM-Compression

Working code for compression experiments: layer pruning, healing fine-tuning, decoder search, and attention-head merging. The goal is to test structural changes and recovery procedures, then track perplexity and downstream behavior. The repository is an experimental workbench more than a polished public library.

mmo_tm and related traffic-assignment work

Solver implementations and experiment pipelines for equilibrium traffic assignment on real road graphs. My contribution is in optimization methods, experiment setup, and convergence comparison, not in GIS or frontend systems.

Resource allocation project

A learned component evaluated against a strong deterministic baseline under external simulation constraints. The main engineering work is preparing features, training loops, evaluation runs, and comparison reports that can be checked by a partner team.
