Igor Ignashin

Research Engineer / LLM Post-Training — distillation, SFT, evaluation, multi-agent RL

I work on LLM post-training and applied research: SFT, teacher/KL distillation, reward and grader parsing, benchmark evaluation, and multi-agent preference-learning setups.

My strongest LLM case is a Qwen/DeepScaleR math-distillation project: 40.3k teacher-generated CoT traces, SFT/Forward-KL training, evaluation on five math benchmarks, and macro Pass@1 improvement from 46.97 to 54.45 against a 55.90 local RL teacher.

At MIPT AI Institute / BRAIn Lab I worked as a Research Team Lead: I led small research teams on distillation/SFT, LLM compression, multi-agent RL/NLHF, SGD dynamics, and optimization projects.

I am an M.S. candidate at MIPT, Department of Intelligent Data Analysis. My research background in optimization, stochastic dynamics, and minimax methods helps me test new LLM quality, alignment, post-training, and inference ideas with controlled experiments rather than intuition alone.
